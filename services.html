Our services

Support for every step, in one place

Everything below can be arranged by the same dedicated person, so you never have to explain your situation twice. If you are not sure what you need, that is alright too, we will talk it through with you.

Categories of support

Getting to know you and planning ahead

We start by getting to know you and your family, in person, at your pace. No forms to fill in before we have even said hello.

  • In-person discussions with you and your family, to build a personal relationship and a tailored approach that respects and honours all wishes
  • Encouraging open and free discussion
  • Bereavement planning
  • Discussions regarding funeral cost and money available

Capturing memories

Memories do not have to fade into paperwork. We help you capture and hold onto what matters, in whatever form feels right.

  • Videos and voice recordings
  • Photo books
  • A Good Farewell Box, to store and share memories, physical and digital
  • Celebration of a loved one and their life

Getting your affairs in order

Plain English support with the paperwork and decisions that protect your family later, gathered securely, and explained without jargon.

  • Securely gathering and storing information in good time: utilities, bills, mortgage, mobile phone, logins, social media accounts, key passwords and accounts
  • Information gathered on assets, debts, estate administration, closing accounts, managing property, and paying taxes
  • Lasting Power of Attorney
  • Life insurance, critical illness cover and income protection arrangements
  • Will writing, review and management
  • Estate planning
  • Information on trusted local businesses for trust planning and administration
  • Arranging payment of debts
  • Charitable donations

Funeral and remembrance

A funeral is a chance to say goodbye in a way that feels true to the person you loved. We help make the arrangements as individual as you would like.

  • Arranging funeral directors
  • Funeral and remembrance arrangements, made as individual as you would like
  • Notifying friends and family of end of life or death
  • Arrangements for people unable to attend the funeral
  • Spreading of ashes
  • Registering the death
  • Unusual arrangements, including dedicating a body to medical science
  • Organ donation

Probate and practical support

The legal and practical side of a death can feel overwhelming on top of grief. Our probate specialist and practical support take the weight off.

  • Probate specialist to navigate probate for you
  • House cleaning
  • House clearance, and valuation or auction where appropriate
  • Access to bereavement support services

Caring for those left behind

Pets are family too. We help make sure they are not forgotten.

  • Arrangements for pets to be cared for
