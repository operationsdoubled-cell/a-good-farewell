Frequently asked questions

Straightforward answers, in plain English. If your question is not here, just ask us directly, there is no such thing as a silly question.

Questions

A Good Farewell is an end-of-life planning and bereavement support service. We are for anyone who wants to plan ahead sensibly, and for families who are coping with a death right now. One dedicated person supports you through the practical and emotional work involved, from wills and probate to funerals and keeping memories alive.

Not at all. Most families come to us with nothing in place, and that is completely normal. We start with a conversation in person to understand where things stand, then help you build things up gently, one step at a time, at whatever pace feels right.

We publish starting prices for our most requested individual services on our pricing page, and every price there is exclusive of VAT. For anything bespoke, we always agree a clear, fixed price with you before any work begins, so there are never any hidden extras or surprise invoices.

Package one is for planning ahead, a full package of assistance before a death. Package two covers involvement on death, administration and funeral arrangements once a loss has happened. Package three is bespoke, combining exactly the elements you need, whatever stage you are at. We will help you work out which fits, or build something in between.

Yes. Many of the families we support come to us without a will in place. We can guide you through the process that applies when someone dies without one, and help going forward with will writing for the rest of the family, so this becomes easier to face next time.

Our probate specialist navigates the probate process on your behalf, from start to finish, while your dedicated point of contact continues to support you and your family with everything else. You will never be passed between departments, it stays personal throughout.
